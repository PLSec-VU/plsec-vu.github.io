Robin Webbers and Luís Ferreirinha presented their works, [Pantomime](https://doi.org/10.1145/3808340) and [zkTAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/TODO), respectively, at the 21st Workshop on Programming Languages and Analysis for Security (PLAS26).



[Pantomime](https://doi.org/10.1145/3808340), work by [Robin Webbers](/people/robin-webbers/), [Robert Schenck](https://rschenck.com), [Wind Wong](/people/wind-wong/), [Kristina Sojakova](/people/kristina-sojakova/), and [Klaus von Gleissenthall](/people/klaus-v-gleissenthall/), previously accepted at PLDI 2026, introduces simulation-based leakage proofs that show a leakage description correctly captures a hardware design. 

![Pantomime](/assets/img/news/pantomime-plas26.jpg)



[zkTAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/TODO), work by [Luís Ferreirinha](/people/luis-ferreirinha/) and [Klaus von Gleissenthall](/people/klaus-v-gleissenthall/), proves memory-safety of proprietary binaries via a typed assembly language, without leaking typing derivations. This work has been submitted to PLDI 2027.
